# Code of Conduct

ChromaDNA is an open project. We want participation to be respectful, constructive, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and technical trade-offs without personal attacks.
- Accept good-faith corrections and differing viewpoints.
- Give attribution for work, data, and ideas that came from others.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's photographs, RAW files, credentials, or private information.
- Keep feedback focused on the work rather than assumptions about the contributor.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, insults, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated disruptive conduct, bad-faith reviews, or knowingly misleading claims about another contributor's work.
- Submitting material that infringes copyright or violates a source license.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when conduct violates this policy.

To report a conduct concern, contact the repository maintainers privately through the GitHub organization or maintainer profile contact channel where available. Do not post personal information or sensitive details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities should be reported through the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.
